BUDGET AND PLANNER · STEP-BY-STEP

How This Planner Works

The Core Idea: Most budgeting apps fail ADHD minds by asking you to track 50 rigid categories, inducing guilt when plans change. Budget and Planner throws all that out and focuses on One True Number: Safe to Spend Today. Everything counts down from real money you actually have.
1
Put in what's actually in Checking
Open your mobile banking app, see what is in your daily checking account right now, and enter it into Budget and Planner. We never guess the future — every calculation starts from real money you actually own today.
2
Set up your Paychecks & Cycle
Set when your next paycheck lands and your pay frequency (monthly, biweekly, or weekly). The app counts down the remaining days so you always know how long your money needs to stretch.
3
Add your Recurring Bills & Must-Pays
Enter rent, utilities, subscriptions, and debts. Any bill due before your next payday is automatically set aside and locked away, so you never accidentally spend rent on daily treats.
4
Wake up & check "Safe to Spend" on Today
Check in every morning. The big number on your Today dashboard tells you exactly what is safe to spend today without jeopardizing upcoming bills.
Transparent Formula:
Checking Balance − Bills Due Before Payday − Annual Sinking Reserves − Today's Spends = Safe to Spend
5
Log Spends in 2 Taps (Low Friction)
Whenever you buy something, open the keypad in the sidebar or tap the header button. Enter the amount or tap quick-steppers (+5, +10, +20) and pick a category. Done in 3 seconds, your safe balance updates instantly.
6
Envelopes for Flexible Limits (Optional)
Want to keep an eye on coffee, dining out, or online shopping? Set flexible monthly envelopes in the Envelopes tab to pace category spending without rigid restrictions.
🛡️ 3 Golden Rules for Peace of Mind
No Double Counting When you update your bank balance, past logged spends and already-paid bills are never deducted twice (smart timestamp tracking).
100% Private & Offline Zero bank linking or login credentials required. Your data stays entirely in your browser with easy JSON/CSV export backups.
Zero Guilt Pacing If you overspend today, your remaining allowance recalculates smoothly across future days without guilt trips.
